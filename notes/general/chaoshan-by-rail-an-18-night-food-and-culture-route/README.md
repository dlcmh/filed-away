## User

coming from Shenzhen (深圳, Shēnzhèn) via HSR, are there HSR stations in each of Jieyang (揭阳, Jiēyáng), Chaozhou (潮州, Cháozhōu) & Shantou (汕头, Shàntóu) that I can arrive in and return to Shenzhen from?

---

## DeepSeek

Yes, you can travel directly by high-speed rail from Shenzhen (深圳, Shēnzhèn) to Jieyang (揭阳, Jiēyáng), Chaozhou (潮州, Cháozhōu), and Shantou (汕头, Shàntóu), and there are stations in each city for both arrival and return journeys.

However, it is important to note that in the Chaoshan (潮汕, Cháoshàn) region, **"Chaozhou" does not have a station named "Chaozhou Station"** on the high-speed rail line. The station serving Chaozhou city is **"Chaoshan Station"** (located in Chaozhou, but named Chaoshan because it serves the three cities jointly). For Jieyang and Shantou, there are separate stations.

### 💡 Practical Tips

When searching for tickets (for example, on the 12306 official website or app), **when heading to Chaozhou, search for "Chaoshan"** as your destination or departure station. Multiple direct trains operate between Shenzhen North Station (深圳北站, Shēnzhèn Běizhàn) and these stations every day, with journey times generally ranging from 1.5 to 2.5 hours, making travel quite convenient.[[1]](https://trains.ctrip.com/TrainSchedule/G8483)[[8]](https://www.crecc.com/huoche/d7345.html)[[14]](https://www.crecc.com/huoche/d7431.html)

## User

I have a total of 18 nights to split among Jieyang (揭阳, Jiēyáng), Chaozhou (潮州, Cháozhōu) and Shantou (汕头, Shàntóu) - what would you suggest in terms of foodie and culture-oriented routes & accommodation

---

## DeepSeek

With 18 nights, you have the luxury of truly slowing down and experiencing the Chaoshan (潮汕, Cháoshàn) region like a local, rather than rushing through the typical highlights. Since you're arriving and departing via high-speed rail from Shenzhen (深圳, Shēnzhèn), a logical and efficient route would be to start in **Jieyang (揭阳, Jiēyáng)** (the northernmost and quietest), move to **Chaozhou (潮州, Cháozhōu)** (the cultural heart), and finish in **Shantou (汕头, Shàntóu)** (the coastal food capital), or vice versa.

Given your focus on food and culture, I suggest allocating your nights roughly as follows: **Jieyang (4 nights), Chaozhou (6 nights), and Shantou (8 nights)**. This gives you ample time for deep food exploration, day trips, and a relaxed pace.

### 🗺️ Suggested Route & Night Allocation

**1. Jieyang: The Authentic, Slow-Paced Start (4 Nights)**\
Jieyang is less touristy and quieter than its neighbors, making it a perfect place to ease into the Chaoshan rhythm and enjoy authentic local life.[[1]](https://in.trip.com/moments/detail/chaozhou-467-157836253?locale=en-IN)

- **Culture & Vibe**: Focus on the well-preserved **Ancient City (揭阳古城, Jiēyáng Gǔchéng)** area. Visit the **Jinxian Gate (进贤门, Jìnxián Mén)** (a Ming Dynasty city gate), the **Jieyang Confucian Academy (揭阳学宫, Jiēyáng Xuégōng)** — the largest Confucius temple in Lingnan (岭南, Lǐngnán) — and the **City God Temple (城隍庙, Chénghuáng Miào)** with its exquisite roof decorations. The old streets of **Zhongshan Road (中山路, Zhōngshān Lù)** offer a mix of Lingnan and Nanyang (南洋, Nányáng)-style architecture.[[4]](https://in.trip.com/moments/detail/jieyang-835-145571042?locale=en-IN#1)[[6]](https://us.trip.com/moments/detail/jieyang-835-142533749/#1)[[13]](https://in.trip.com/moments/detail/nan-ao-county-958-157343544)
- **Food Focus**: This is your chance to try dishes without the crowds. The must-eat dishes include **pork trotter rice (猪脚饭, zhūjiǎo fàn)**, **Puning dried tofu (普宁豆干, Pǔníng dòugān)**, **kway chap (粿汁, guǒzhī)**, and local **beef hotpot (牛肉火锅, niúròu huǒguō)**. For a unique experience, try the late-night **oyster omelet (蚝烙, háolào)** (using duck eggs) or explore the **Lu Qian Night Market (卢前夜市, Lúqián Yèshì)**.[[1]](https://in.trip.com/moments/detail/chaozhou-467-157836253?locale=en-IN)[[6]](https://us.trip.com/moments/detail/jieyang-835-142533749/#1)
- **Accommodation**: Stay near **Jinxian Gate** or the **Ancient City** in Rongcheng District (榕城区, Róngchéng Qū). This puts you within walking distance of the main sights and food spots. Look for boutique homestays in old courtyard houses for atmosphere, or modern hotels near Wanda Plaza (万达广场, Wàndá Guǎngchǎng) for convenience.[[6]](https://us.trip.com/moments/detail/jieyang-835-142533749/#1)[[12]](https://pk.trip.com/moments/detail/jieyang-835-156708686)

**2. Chaozhou: The Cultural Immersion (6 Nights)**\
Chaozhou is the cultural soul of the region. With six nights, you can thoroughly explore the ancient city and even take a side trip.

- **Culture & Vibe**: Immerse yourself in the **Ancient City (潮州古城, Cháozhōu Gǔchéng)**. Walk **Paifang Street (牌坊街, Páifāng Jiē)** with its 23 Ming and Qing archways, visit the **Kaiyuan Temple (开元寺, Kāiyuán Sì)**, and walk across the famous **Guangji Bridge (广济桥, Guǎngjì Qiáo)** (try to catch the daily "bridge opening" and the evening light show). Don't miss the quieter **Han Yu Memorial Hall (韩文公祠, Hánwéngōng Cí)** for views, and consider a day trip to **Longhu Ancient Village (龙湖古寨, Lónghú Gǔzhài)**.[[2]](https://discoverguangdong.com/recommended-itineraries/dtdlpdzqldpcp7isikmz9y2y)[[8]](https://www.chinahighlights.com/chaozhou-shantou/top-things-to-do.htm#1)
- **Food Focus**: Chaozhou is a snack paradise. Beyond the main street, explore **Xima Road (西马路, Xīmǎ Lù)** for **oyster omelets**, **Yamuniang (鸭母捻, yā mǔ niǎn)** (sweet soup balls), and **fermented bean curd chicken wings (腐乳鸡翅, fǔrǔ jīchì)**. For main meals, try **Chaozhou cuisine (潮州菜, Cháozhōu cài)** (like braised goose), **beef hotpot**, and **Phoenix Dancong tea (凤凰单丛, Fènghuáng Dāncóng)**.[[1]](https://in.trip.com/moments/detail/chaozhou-467-157836253?locale=en-IN)[[2]](https://discoverguangdong.com/recommended-itineraries/dtdlpdzqldpcp7isikmz9y2y)[[5]](https://pk.trip.com/moments/detail/chaozhou-467-154109285)
- **Accommodation**: Stay **inside or immediately adjacent to the Ancient City**. Being near **Paifang Street** or **West Lake (西湖, Xīhú)** allows you to wander out for late-night snacks and early morning strolls before the day-trippers arrive. Options range from lake-view inns to modern boutique hotels.[[5]](https://pk.trip.com/moments/detail/chaozhou-467-154109285)[[10]](https://in.trip.com/moments/detail/chaozhou-467-157655381?locale=en-IN)

**3. Shantou: The Coastal Food Finale (8 Nights)**\
Shantou is a vibrant blend of port-city history and incredible seafood. The extra nights here allow for a relaxed exploration of the old town and a trip to an island.

- **Culture & Vibe**: The **Small Park (小公园, Xiǎo Gōngyuán)** area is a must, with its radiating arcade buildings offering a strong retro atmosphere. Explore the **Opening Port Cultural Exhibition Hall (开埠文化陈列馆, Kāibù Wénhuà Chénlièguǎn)** and the **General Post Office (邮政总局, Yóuzhèng Zǒngjú)**. For a change of scenery, dedicate a day or two to **Nan'ao Island (南澳岛, Nán'ào Dǎo)** for coastal drives, lighthouses, and seafood.[[1]](https://in.trip.com/moments/detail/chaozhou-467-157836253?locale=en-IN)[[8]](https://www.chinahighlights.com/chaozhou-shantou/top-things-to-do.htm#1)
- **Food Focus**: This is the place to indulge in **beef hotpot** (a local obsession), **raw marinated seafood (生腌, shēngyān)** (start with a small portion if you're unaccustomed), and **braised goose (卤鹅, lǔ é)**. **Longyan South Road (龙眼南路, Lóngyǎn Nán Lù)** is a major food street, and you can find excellent **rice noodle rolls (肠粉, chángfěn)** and **tofu pudding (豆花, dòuhuā)** throughout the old town.[[3]](https://nz.trip.com/moments/poi-queshi-scenic-spot-79922/#2)
- **Accommodation**: Stay near **Small Park** for maximum immersion in the old-town atmosphere and easy access to food (ideal for night owls). Alternatively, the area around **Longyan South Road** or **MixC (万象城, Wànxiàngchéng)** offers more modern conveniences and is still close to the action.[[11]](https://www.trip.com/moments/detail/shantou-215-153987199/)

### 💡 Practical Tips

- **High-Speed Rail**: Your plan to use HSR is perfect. The stations are **Jieyang Station (揭阳站, Jiēyáng Zhàn)**, **Chaoshan Station (潮汕站, Cháoshàn Zhàn)** (for Chaozhou), and **Shantou Station (汕头站, Shàntóu Zhàn)**. The inter-city train rides are short (around 20-40 minutes), making transfers easy.
- **Food Strategy**: Pace yourself. Chaoshan food is rich. Mix heavy meals (hotpot, braised goose) with lighter snacks and plenty of **Kung Fu tea (工夫茶, gōngfu chá)** to aid digestion. Be cautious with raw marinated seafood if you have a sensitive stomach; eat it with plain congee (白粥, báizhōu) and in moderation.[[2]](https://discoverguangdong.com/recommended-itineraries/dtdlpdzqldpcp7isikmz9y2y)
- **Day Trips**: Your generous timeline allows for spontaneous detours, like exploring the **Chen Cihong Former Residence (陈慈黉故居, Chén Cíhóng Gùjū)** (a stunning Nanyang-style mansion) near Shantou or checking out the **Huangmanzhai Waterfall (黄满寨瀑布, Huángmǎnzhài Pùbù)** if you want a nature break from Jieyang.[[8]](https://www.chinahighlights.com/chaozhou-shantou/top-things-to-do.htm#1)[[9]](https://www.trip.com/moments/poi-city-152805611#2)

This route lets you gradually shift from the quiet authenticity of Jieyang to the cultural depth of Chaozhou, and finally to the energetic, food-obsessed coastal vibe of Shantou. Enjoy your extended foodie and culture adventure!
